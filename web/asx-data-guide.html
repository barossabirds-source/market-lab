ASX overnight study: collecting usable evidence

Return to the study

Automatic research collection captures US closes and selected ASX ETF minute bars after market close. SPI futures and verified executable quote/auction data are not connected. Automatic minute bars remain private and do not populate this strict CSV study. Import verified observations from a provider whose licence permits this use. Daily closing prices cannot substitute for intraday entries. The empty template contains no invented market data.

The fixed research rule

At 9:30 am Australia/Sydney time, the latest completed S&P 500 session must be positive and SPI futures must show a change of at least +0.5%. Record the exact futures contract and previous settlement reference in your source archive; use a consistent contract-roll method. Buy the same Australian ETF at 10:05 am and sell at 3:55 pm. Use the first executable quote at the specified instant, or a consistently documented execution estimate, and allow for spread and slippage. A bar's later closing price cannot be treated as its starting price.

Times are Sydney exchange time, not Adelaide time. Use explicit UTC offsets, including daylight saving. Dates before 16 October 2026 are exploratory. Later dates fall in the prospective rule period, but an import alone does not prove the data were unseen.

CSV fields

FieldRequired contents
session_dateAustralian trading date, YYYY-MM-DD. One row per observed session, including non-signal sessions. Omit exchange holidays.
symbolOne consistently used Australian ETF identifier, such as A200.AX. This is an identifier example, not a recommendation.
us_return_pctLatest completed S&P 500 close-to-close percentage change. Enter 1.2 for +1.2%, not 0.012.
us_close_atActual timestamp of that US close. It must precede the Australian signal and be less than 96 hours old; verify US holidays separately.
spi_change_pctSPI futures percentage change from the documented prior settlement. Enter 0.5 for +0.5%.
spi_observed_at; signal_atBoth must be 09:30:00 Sydney time on the session date. Do not use the overnight high or a later morning quote.
previous_close; open_priceSame ETF's previous trading close and current opening auction price in AUD. Use a consistent price basis and review splits and distribution dates. These calculate the opening gap only.
open_atActual ETF opening-auction timestamp, between 10:00:00 and 10:05:00 Sydney time. If the ETF has not opened by entry, exclude the row.
entry_price; entry_atETF price at 10:05:00 Sydney time on the session date. Positive AUD price.
exit_price; exit_atETF price at 15:55:00 Sydney time that day. Positive AUD price. Incomplete sessions are rejected.
sourceOne consistent provider/method name per import. Retain original quotes, acquisition times, futures contracts, settlement references and any execution assumptions separately.

All timestamps require ISO format with seconds and timezone, for example 2026-10-16T09:30:00+11:00. Use quoted CSV fields if a source description contains commas. Rejected rows are listed in the study and do not enter results. Check rejection counts for selection bias.

Reading the results

Net A$ = research amount × (exit price ÷ entry price − 1) − round-trip brokerage − research amount × round-trip spread/slippage basis points ÷ 10,000. Ten basis points is 0.10%. Default costs are illustrative assumptions.

Compare signal days with other observed days, and with all observed days under identical trade times and costs. Import a complete consecutive period; a cherry-picked file can produce misleading comparisons. The table reports sample count, average net result, proportion positive, average winning/losing trade, worst trade and largest peak-to-trough fall in the running sum of fixed-size trades. It does not annualise returns or calculate portfolio performance.

Results do not control for other news, volatility, currency or common causes. Positive averages and a high win rate do not establish causation or guarantee future returns. Review several years and a genuinely untouched test period, then capture new sessions without changing the rule.
