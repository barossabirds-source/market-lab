A$100 default: pair trades use total gross exposure split equally between the long and short sides. A short position may require additional broker collateral.

Important: these are gross historical illustrations, not forecasts. They exclude brokerage, bid/ask spread, short-borrow costs, foreign-exchange conversion, tax and slippage. HOW THE PATTERN CHANGES

Longitudinal Trends

How to read this: the tariff table uses the same pre-registered high-surprise escalation rule each time. The running sum adds repeated fixed-size historical examples; it is not a portfolio backtest, because events can overlap and real trading costs are excluded. A weaker result in later periods can suggest novelty decay, but it does not prove that repeated announcements caused the change.

SECTOR DISPERSION

Policy Impact Map

Shows what happened after an event across market areas. Movement is compared with the broad US market and with the same period before the event.

Market area Fund Fund change Broad market change Difference from broad market Difference before event Change after event compared with before Timing quality

Positive and negative signs are shown as well as colour. Displayed figures are rounded, so subtracting two displayed numbers can differ slightly from the stored result. A difference is an observed pattern, not proof that the policy event caused it. Intraday periods such as 30 minutes and two hours are deliberately left out until reliable intraday data is captured consistently.

PRE-REGISTER BEFORE TESTING

Research Trial Register

A hypothesis is written down before its result is inspected. This helps stop accidental cherry-picking.

Promotion rule: a trial stays out of simulated strategy testing until it shows repeated support across at least 20 qualifying events and then holds up on data that was not used to judge the idea.

ONLY AFTER THE EVIDENCE

Simulated Strategy Gate

1Register the idea

State the rule before testing it.

2Repeated historical tests

Check different periods and trading costs.

3Unseen-data test

Use historical or later data that was not used to choose or judge the rule.

4New-data simulation

Record what the fixed rule would do on genuinely new data.

No live trading connection. Market Lab does not place brokerage orders. A strategy only reaches simulation after passing the research gates.
